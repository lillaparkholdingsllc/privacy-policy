PRIVACY POLICY — PENNYRA

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Developer: Lilla Park Holdings

App: Pennyra (package name: com.lillaparkholdings.pennyra)

Contact: lillaparkholdingsllc@gmail.com

1. Overview

Pennyra is a personal budget and expense tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Pennyra does not collect, transmit or share your personal information.

2. Information you enter

When you use Pennyra you may enter transaction amounts, categories (including custom categories you create), dates, optional notes, a monthly budget limit and a currency symbol. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage analytics or crash reports. Pennyra does not require an account.

4. Internet access and third parties

Pennyra does not request internet permission and works fully offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

5. Data storage and security

Your data lives in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by the Android operating system's app sandbox. We cannot access it. Please keep your device secure, for example with a screen lock.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual transactions inside the app, or delete all data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling Pennyra. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

7. Backups

Android may include app data in your device or Google account backup, depending on your device settings. Such backups are handled by Google or your device manufacturer under their own policies, not by us.

8. Children's privacy

Pennyra does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Not financial advice

Pennyra is a record-keeping tool. It does not connect to bank accounts, process payments, or provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same location.
